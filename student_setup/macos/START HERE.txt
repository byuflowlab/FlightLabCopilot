FLIGHTLABCOPILOT FOR macOS

1. Double-click "Start FlightLabCopilot.command".
2. Keep the launcher window open while you use FlightLabCopilot in your browser.
3. Click Exit in the app, or close the launcher window, when you are finished.

The first launch needs internet access and may take a few minutes. Later
launches are faster. FlightLabCopilot checks for updates automatically.

If macOS blocks the downloaded file, Control-click "Start FlightLabCopilot.command",
choose Open, and then choose Open again. You normally need to do this only once.

The flying field has no internet, so open it once at home or on campus first.
After that it starts without internet (the update check is skipped).

If setup fails, take a screenshot of the launcher window and send it to your TA.
